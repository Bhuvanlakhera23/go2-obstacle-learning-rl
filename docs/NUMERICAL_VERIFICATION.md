# RC2 numerical invariance

All eleven numerical tables preserve every RC1 cell except the approved public
policy/arm labels. There are 6,912 unique original first-episode records.
Nominal high-obstacle detector-defined completions remain 117/128 for the
Fixed-Mixture Policy, 47/128 for the Adaptive-Frontier Policy and 1/128 for the
historical Pretrained Reference.

All six SVG/PNG pairs are rerendered from these tables with the same underlying
values, denominators, colors and scales. Labels change, not evidence. No new
uncertainty bar, seed, trajectory, physical-contact observation or experiment is
introduced. The scientific body and claim boundaries are preserved under the
documented naming/metadata transformation.

Original checkpoint/source identities remain in the [mapping appendix](POLICY_IDENTITY_MAPPING.md)
and retained logical inventories. [VERIFICATION.json](../VERIFICATION.json) records
table, figure and public file hashes. RC1 remains untouched and is separately
sealed by its original inventory. No GPU or native execution occurs.
